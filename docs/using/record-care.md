---
title: Record care
description: Track cleaning and sleeves for each physical copy.
sidebar:
  order: 5
---

Open an album and find **Copies & care**. Use **Log care** for the default copy, or the action on a specific copy when you own more than one.

Log a cleaning or sleeve change with its date and details. **Add copy** separates a second pressing or duplicate from the first copy's history. Give copies labels you can recognise on the shelf.

The care list shows records needing attention. `RECORD_CLEANING_OVERDUE_DAYS` controls the default overdue threshold. Removing a copy hides it from the list but keeps its history. Void an incorrect care entry rather than logging a second event to compensate.

The [CLI](/docs/cli/) also provides `records` commands for care and copies.
